Our Services

Comprehensive, professional services for children with disabilities and their families.

Welfare Services

Psychological, social, physical, and cognitive development support for children with disabilities.

Professional Assessments

Multi-disciplinary evaluations by Social Workers, Paediatricians, Psychologists, and Therapists.

Parent & Caregiver Training

Workshops on caring for children with cerebral palsy, home stimulation, and ongoing support.

Therapeutic Groups

Support for children with learning barriers and low self-esteem through group facilitation.

Awareness Programmes

Community education on social issues and the rights of children with disabilities.

Family Empowerment

Skills training, support groups, and ongoing assistance for families.
